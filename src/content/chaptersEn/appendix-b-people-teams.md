This appendix lists the people, teams, and knowledge sources indispensable for understanding the FDE model. For the complete list of sources referenced during the writing of this book, see the research notes published alongside the manuscript.

## I. Key Figures

**Shyam Sankar** — President and Chief Technology Officer of Palantir, early employee. Widely regarded as the inventor of the FDE strategy: the one who redefined "on-site customization" from a cost into "product discovery." His public statements are first-hand material for understanding Palantir's philosophy.

**Bob McGrew** — Early engineer at PayPal, early Palantir executive, former Chief Research Officer of OpenAI (led the development of ChatGPT, GPT-4, and o1). The best exponent of the FDE model. The "gravel roads and highways" analogy and "doing things that don't scale at scale" both come from him. Required reading: the YC Lightcone podcast episode *The FDE Playbook for AI Startups* (September 2025).

**Stephen Cohen** — Co-founder of Palantir. The inventor of the demo loop: the protagonist of "This thing is terrible" and "So what would you want to be different?"

**Alex Karp** — Chief Executive Officer of Palantir. The originator of the "French waiter" behavioral model: an opponent of the compliant, order-taking engineering culture.

**Barry** — Former Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer, author of the essay *Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering*. The most important "insider warning" on the FDE model: a true accounting of cost, chaos, and burnout.

**Ted Mabrey / Nabeel Qureshi** — Writers from the Palantir orbit, with deep public writing on how Palantir operates.

**Colin Jarvis** — Head of OpenAI's forward deployed engineering team, the person who built OpenAI's FDE organization from scratch.

**Brad Lightcap** — Chief Operating Officer of OpenAI, the leading force behind the "deployment company."

**Natalie Meurer** — Head of agent engineering at Sierra (five years at Palantir prior). The one who coined the name "Agent Engineer." Required reading: the Latent Space podcast interview (July 2026).

**Bret Taylor / Clay Bavor** — Founders of Sierra (the former was co-CEO of Salesforce, the former a Vice President at Google). The ones who turned the FDE model into a business model in itself (outcome-based pricing).

**Jesse Zhang / Ashwin Sreenivas** — Founders of Decagon (the latter came out of Palantir). Designers of the agent operating procedure, the template for "productizing delivery experience."

**David Wakeling** — Former head of market innovation at Linklaters, the in-house supporter of Harvey's first lighthouse deployment. The best sample of the client-side supporter perspective.

## II. Signature Teams

**Palantir FDE organization** — The inventor and final form of the model. The "Echo-Delta-Platform" triangle, the Bootcamp machine.

**OpenAI forward deployed engineering team** — Formed in 2024, globally distributed, the operators behind John Deere and BBVA; upgraded into the "deployment company" in 2026.

**Anthropic applied AI team** — An FDE organization built under the name "Applied AI Engineer," co-builder of the FIS financial-crime agent; in 2026 formed an enterprise AI services joint venture with Blackstone and others.

**Sierra agent engineering team** — The extreme form of outcome-based pricing plus managed delivery (founded in 2023, valued at $4.5 billion in October 2024, and reportedly continued to climb thereafter).

**Harvey deployment team** — The textbook of vertical-industry (legal) FDE (founded in 2022, valued at $11 billion in March 2026, with users covering 100,000+ lawyers).

**Scale AI / Databricks / Salesforce / Google Cloud / Mistral / Cohere** — Each has set up FDE or isomorphic roles under different names (solutions architect, customer engineer, etc.), with the same skeleton.

**Volcano Engine Doubao large model FDE team** — The most explicit FDE organization among China's internet giants (established in 2026, covering automotive, healthcare, education, finance, and semiconductors; co-building a thousand-person-scale team with EY).

**China's homegrown FDE pioneers** — Local service providers anchored in property facility management and the financial industry, who have publicly articulated the defining thinking that "FDE is not the same as on-site outsourcing."

## III. Knowledge Sources (Ranked by Importance)

1. YC Lightcone podcast: *The FDE Playbook for AI Startups—A Bob McGrew Interview* (September 2025) — a 51-minute recap from the model's best storyteller.
2. a16z: *Trading Margin for Moat* — the benchmark for business-model analysis.
3. Barry: *Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering* — a cool-headed insider warning.
4. MIT NANDA Lab: *The GenAI Divide: 2025 State of Business AI* — the source of the "95% failure rate," the data foundation for the FDE rationale.
5. The New Stack: *Why OpenAI and Anthropic Are Both Racing to Build FDE Teams* (May 2026).
6. CIO.com: *Anthropic's Finance Agent Exposes a New Bottleneck—the Forward Deployed Engineer* (May 2026) — the buyer's perspective and the Gartner warning.
7. Latent Space podcast: *Forward Deployed Engineers and the Future of Software Engineering* (July 2026) — the Sierra perspective.
8. Palantir official blog: *A Day in the Life of a Palantir Forward Deployed Engineer* (2020) — an official self-account, with a Chinese translation in circulation.
9. OpenFDE community (open-fde.com) — the open-source community of practitioners, with a map of roles and companies.
10. 36Kr's *China's To-B Software Escaping the Trap of Loss-Making Growth*, and Chen Guo George's FDE series on WeChat — required reading for the Chinese context.

## IV. Compensation and Job-Hunting Reference

- GetPerspective: *2026 Forward Deployed Engineer Compensation Report* (1,200 samples) — at top labs, the median total annual comp for a mid-level FDE is about $385,000, senior about $610,000, and principal over $1.2 million.
- fdenest.com / fde.academy / sundeepteki.org — three dedicated sites for interview prep and career paths.
- Levels.fyi — real-time compensation data for positions at Palantir and others.
- Job postings themselves are the best textbook: the FDE postings of OpenAI, Anthropic, Decagon, Harvey, and Scale AI are worth studying word for word.
